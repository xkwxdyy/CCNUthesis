% 中文摘要
\begin{abstract}
  《普通高中数学课程标准-2017年版》把数学作为一门重要的学科素质，是一门最基础的数学知识。数学运算是用来进行推理的，它是用来求解问题的。在中学阶段，数学计算的水平已逐渐上升到了一个很大的程度，但是目前西藏地区的中学生的数学运算水平却不容乐观。因此，要弄清哪些因素会影响到学生的数学运算能力、在课堂上应注意的问题以及怎样提高他们的数学计算能力。

  学习数学，并不是单纯的数学，而是要了解和思考。第三章为调查研究的结论和分析，从问卷调查、试卷错误分析、教师访谈等方面对西藏高中学生目前的数学计算水平进行了调查，从学生、教师和教学环境三个方面对西藏高中学生的数学运算能力产生了一定的影响。第四章从西藏高中学生的数学运算能力的影响因子分析。第五章从西藏高中学生的数学计算能力的影响因素出发，并提出相应的对策。
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{abstract*}
  Mathematics curriculum standards for senior high schools - 2017  Edition takes mathematics as an important subject quality, which is the most basic mathematics knowledge.Mathematical operation is used for reasoning. It is used to solve problems. In the middle school stage, the level of mathematical calculation has gradually risen to a great extent, but at present, the level of mathematical calculation of middle school students in Tibet is not optimistic. Therefore, we should understand what factors play an important role in students' mathematical operation level, how to pay attention to what in class and how to improve their mathematical calculation level. Learning mathematics is not simple mathematics, but to understand and think. The second chapter is the definition of high school students' mathematical operation and the significance of cultivating Tibetan high school students' mathematical operation. The third chapter is the conclusion and analysis of the investigation and research. It investigates the current mathematical calculation level of Tibetan high school students from the aspects of questionnaire survey, test paper error analysis and teacher interview. It has a certain impact on the mathematical calculation ability of Tibetan high school students from three aspects: students, teachers and teaching environment. The fourth chapter analyzes the influencing factors of Tibetan high school students' mathematical operation ability. The fifth chapter puts forward measures to protect the mathematical operation ability of Tibetan senior high school students from the influencing factors.
\end{abstract*}
