% 中文摘要
\begin{abstract}
  习题课是不可或缺的高中数学课型之一，探究性学习是高效学习的良好方法，因此高中数学习题课中的探究性学习具有极大重要性。本文根据数学习题课的教学模式的存在问题，为习题课提供新的教学与学习方法即探究性学习，结合选定的课题《函数的性质——函数的凹凸性》设计探究性教学方案，由实验学生与教师的反馈得出该案例教学三维目标的实现较为良好，且学生主动性和课堂积极性的提升是该实例的重要成效。
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{abstract*}
  Exercises’ lessons are one of the most important senior mathematics lessons, while inquiry-based learning is a good way to learn mathematics efficiently. Therefore, inquiry-based learning in senior mathematics exercises’ lessons is of great importance. Based on the existence of the teaching mode of the senior mathematics exercises’ lessons, the new model of teaching and learning such as inquiry-based learning are given, which is combined with the selected project "The Nature of Functions - The Convexity of Functions". According to the experimental students and teachers’ feedback, the realization in the three-dimensional goal of the project is relatively good. And the improvement of student activity is also an important result of this project.
\end{abstract*}
