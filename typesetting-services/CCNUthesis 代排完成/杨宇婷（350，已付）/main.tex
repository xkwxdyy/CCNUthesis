% 中文摘要
\begin{abstract}
  由于其具有丰富的知识背景和意义以及在高中数学解题中的广泛使用，导数成为了高中数学大题的又一创新点。又因为导数在高中数学阶段与函数、几何等内容都有交织，因此在知识的深度和广度上，它都是一个很好的数学解题工具。本文研究的主要目的是希望学生能在理解导数的基本概念和基础解题方法后拓宽、深化导数在高中数学中的综合应用，提高运用导数解决难题的能力，进而更好地为未来的数学学习打下基础。最后，基于本研究的发现，对高中导数的解题应用提供一些教学建议。
\end{abstract}

% 英文摘要
\begin{abstract*}
  Due to its extensive use of knowledge background and significance and wide use in high school mathematics solutions, the derivative has become another new point in high school mathematics. Also, because the derivatives are intertwined in high school mathematics stages and functions, geometric and other content are interleaving, so it is a good mathematical solution tool in the depth and breadth of knowledge.The main purpose of this paper is to hope that students can broaden and deepen the comprehensive application of derivative in senior high school mathematics after understanding the basic concepts and basic problem-solving methods of derivatives, improving the ability to use derivative to solve problems, and then better lay a foundation for future mathematics learning.Finally, based on this study, some teaching suggestions are provided for the problem-solving application of derivative in senior high school.
\end{abstract*}
